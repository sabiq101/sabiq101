About me

I connect material-level discovery to device and system-level impact.

I completed my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering Honors at The University of Texas at Austin and am continuing at UT Austin for my PhD in ECE. I conduct research in the Integrated Nano-Computing Lab, where I work on magnetic tunnel junctions, magnetoresistive memory, and AI-assisted materials discovery.

My broader interests include physics-informed machine learning, first-principles simulation, neuromorphic computing, scientific computing, and hardware for intelligent edge systems.

LEAD: literature-enhanced materials discovery for magnetic tunnel junctions.

I developed a workflow combining natural-language processing and large language models with first-principles electronic-structure and quantum-transport calculations to identify promising material strategies for high-performance MTJs.
