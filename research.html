Research

Materials, devices, and computational tools for next-generation computing.

My research lies at the intersection of spintronics, electronic materials, first-principles modeling, machine learning, and computer engineering.

01

Magnetic devices

Spintronics and magnetic tunnel junctions

I investigate material and structural approaches for improving magnetic tunnel junction performance, with emphasis on tunnel magnetoresistance, resistance-area tradeoffs, interfacial layers, magnetic anisotropy, and experimentally realizable stacks.

  • MTJs
  • MRAM
  • Spin transport
  • Sputtering
  • VSM
  • AFM
02

Materials informatics

AI-assisted materials discovery

My LEAD workflow uses scientific literature to generate and prioritize materials hypotheses, then evaluates them with density functional theory and quantum-transport simulations. The goal is to make materials search more targeted, explainable, and directly connected to device metrics.

  • MaterialsBERT
  • LLaMA
  • Word2Vec
  • QuantumATK
  • DFT
03

Physics-informed learning

Machine learning for ferroelectric materials

I am developing computational methods that combine literature context, equivariant crystal graph networks, and first-principles validation to predict tensorial ferroelectric observables and prioritize promising functional materials.

  • Equivariant GNNs
  • Polarization
  • Born charges
  • DFT+U+V
  • Quantum ESPRESSO
